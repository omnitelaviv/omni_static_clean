Spectral-Domain Phase Microscopy (SDPM)

  • A single-point fiber-optics IPM system which is based on spectral-domain optical coherence microscopy.
  • Can achieve several tens of picometers optical thickness accuracy in time.
  • System is portable, inexpensive and does not require an optical table.
  • System has a great potential for clinical applications.

Applications:

•Red blood cells (RBCs):
 Measuring membrane fluctuations.
•Neurons: Measuring action potentials.
  I. Shock, A. Barbul, P. Girshovitz, U. Nevo, R. Korenstein, and N. T. Shaked, “Optical phase measurements in red blood cells using low-coherence spectroscopy,” Journal of Biomedical Optics,  Vol. 17, No. 10, 101509:1-5, 2012 [PDF] [Link].
