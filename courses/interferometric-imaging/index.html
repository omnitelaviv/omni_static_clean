Advanced Interferometric Imaging Methods

.
Course Name: Advanced Interferometric Imaging Methods
.
Lecturer: Dr. Natan T. Shaked
.
Level: Graduate (MSc/PhD/Postdoc) and advanced undergraduate (4th year BSc)
.
Course No: 0553-5512-01
.
Course Grade: 50% final exam, 40% work (including simulations/experiments), 10% presentation
.
Course Subjects:
  • Interferometry and types of interferometers for creating interference.
  • Temporal and spatial coherence.
  • Optical and digital holography.
  • Quantitative phase interferometry.
  • Coherent gating for creating sectioning and 3-D imaging.
  • Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT), types: time and frequency domain, scanning and scan-free (full-field), Doppler.
  • Biomedical and clinical applications.
.
Books:

1. D. Nolte, Bioanalysis: Optical Interferometry for Biology and Medicine, Springer (2012).

2. N. T. Shaked, et al., Biomedical Optical Phase Microscopy and Nanoscopy, Elsevier (2013).

3. J. G. Fujimoto and W. Drexler, Optical Coherence Tomography: Technology and Applications, Springer (2008).
