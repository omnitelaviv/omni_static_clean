The Biomedical Optical Microscopy, Nanoscopy and Interferometry Research Group is a part of the Department of Biomedical Engineering and the Nano Center of Tel Aviv University, Tel Aviv, Israel.

Directed by Prof. Natan T. Shaked and located on three labs spaces, the group performs multidisciplinary research involving optical engineering, imaging and sensing in biological systems, artificial intelligence (AI), and biophysics. We aim at developing new experimental and analytical tools for label-free quantitative cell imaging while harnessing novel AI approaches.
